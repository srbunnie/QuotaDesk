For people who use several AIs

Know how much of your AI you can still use. Without opening any of it.

A tiny animated office sits in the corner of your screen. Each AI gets its own character: it works when the AI works, calls you when the AI needs you, and warns you before the limit runs out.

  • Free
  • Open source
  • Nothing leaves your computer
  • Windows 10 and 11
Install for free See it in action

Works with Codex, Claude Code, Antigravity, OpenRouter, Magnific and Figma.

How it works

Install it and use your AIs as usual. QuotaDesk keeps track on its own. Each AI gets a room, a character and a card with what matters.

What's left

How much you can still use in the next 5 hours and this week, and when the limit resets. For Magnific and OpenRouter, your credit balance.

When it runs out

QuotaDesk follows your pace and works out when the limit will run out. If that's before it resets, you'll know in time to switch AIs.

Who needs you

When an agent asks for permission or has a question, its character calls you and Windows shows a notification. One click opens the list.

What it's for

For people who use several AIs every day and don't want to hit a limit halfway through a task.

01

Never run out of AI mid-task

If you're using it up faster than the limit allows, the card tells you: "at this pace, runs out at 4:08 PM, before it resets." You have time to wrap up calmly or switch AIs.

When the limit runs out, the character takes a break and leaves a sign saying when it will be back.

02

Know right away when an AI needs you

Asked for permission? Has a question? The character stops to think, the card shows how many pending requests there are, and Windows lets you know. Click the notification to see the list.

While they work, the characters tell you what they're doing: "Ran the command", "Editing a file".

03

Pick the best AI for your next task

Codex paused until 6 PM, Claude with plenty left this week, Gemini free in Antigravity: all side by side, with the time each limit resets.

For Magnific and OpenRouter, you see your balance, how much you spend per day and how long it will last.

04

An always-on panel on a tiny screen

Plug in a 3.5" USB-C screen (like the Turing Smart Screen) or a small monitor. QuotaDesk finds it and shows one AI at a time, in landscape or portrait.

An office that follows your day

Pick your city and the windows show the real sky: sunrise, sunshine, rain, storms and the moon in its right phase. When the AIs stop, the characters take a nap.

Your conversations stay with you

QuotaDesk runs entirely on your computer and only looks at what it needs to show limits and status.

Doesn't read your chats

It never reads or sends what you ask your AIs or what they answer. What the characters say comes only from the type of action, like "Ran the command".

Sign-ins kept safe

Your Magnific and OpenRouter sign-ins are encrypted by Windows. Only your account, on this computer, can open them.

No open doors

The link to Claude Code only accepts calls from your own computer. Websites can't talk to it.

QuotaDesk only goes online to read your Magnific and OpenRouter balance, check whether Figma is up and get the weather, if you turn it on. Found a security issue? See how to report it in SECURITY.md.
