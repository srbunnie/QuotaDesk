# Connect Figma

There's nothing to set up: the card shows whether the Figma Agent service is up.

**Shows:** whether the **Figma Agent** service is operational, degraded or down.

## How it works

QuotaDesk reads the public `status.figma.com` page once an hour. This is the global service status: it doesn't check your account's credits or tasks, and it doesn't ask you to sign in.

## If you don't use Figma

Hide the card in **Settings › Tools** by unchecking its name.
